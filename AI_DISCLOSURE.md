# AI disclosure

This is an AI-assisted cryptanalysis project. AI agents may perform target discovery,
attack-history searches, scheme reconstruction, cryptanalytic derivations,
experiments, implementation, adversarial review, documentation, and fresh-context
reproduction.

Independent AI reproduction is labeled as such and is not described as human
verification. A published attack is classified `VERIFIED_ATTACK` after its recorded
technical, adversarial, independent-reproduction, history, and publication-review
gates pass. Human verification is optional additional review rather than a status
gate and is not required for publication. Public materials omit private
chain-of-thought and raw agent scratch work while retaining the evidence, commands,
code, and measurements needed to assess and reproduce technical claims.
