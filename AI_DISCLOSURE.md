# AI disclosure

This is an AI-assisted cryptanalysis project. AI agents may perform target discovery,
attack-history searches, scheme reconstruction, cryptanalytic derivations,
experiments, implementation, adversarial review, documentation, and fresh-context
reproduction.

Independent AI reproduction is labeled as such and is not described as human
verification. Every published attack records whether independent human verification
is pending or complete. Public materials omit private chain-of-thought and raw agent
scratch work while retaining the evidence, commands, code, and measurements needed
to assess and reproduce technical claims.
